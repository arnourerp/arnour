About Arnour SAP Network

Arnour is a dedicated professional network and recruiting marketplace for SAP consultants, architects and enterprise employers.

Skills first, names second

On Arnour every profile and every job is described by SAP product, module and projects delivered. That is enough to judge fit, so identities stay anonymous until both the professional and the company say yes.

Who it is for

  • SAP professionals who want roles that already match their stack, without recruiter noise.
  • Employers and SAP partners who want to search talent by exact product, module, role type, location and availability.

SAP role types

  • Functional Consultant — Configures core business modules and process flows.
  • Developer / Technical Consultant — Builds custom applications, APIs, and integrations.
  • Basis, Cloud & Security — Manages system performance, security, databases, and hosting.
  • Solution & Enterprise Architect — Drives high-level landscape design and governance.
  • Project / Delivery Manager — Directs project timelines, resources, and cutovers.
  • Support & AMS / Application Lead — Oversees day-to-day operations and incident tickets.
  • Business Expert / Key User — In-house business specialist validating requirements.
  • Junior / Trainee — Early-career professional building SAP skills.

How it works

  1. Build an SAP profile. List your products, modules, projects delivered, languages and role type.
  2. Get matched. Roles are ranked against your exact product and module combination.
  3. Reveal on your terms. Companies see your skills first. Your name and CV are shared only after you accept an introduction.
